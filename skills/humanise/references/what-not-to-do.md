# What not to do

- **Don't evade AI detection as the goal.** The goal is voice transfer — authentic-sounding rewriting because the user's voice is actually on it. Better detection scores are a side effect of genuine voice, not the target.
- **Don't over-Australianise.** If the document is a proposal to an American client, dial Aussie idiom down to near-zero and keep Aussie spelling only if the user's context warrants it.
- **Don't delete substance.** Humanisation removes LLM *filler*, not LLM *content*. If the draft makes a specific claim, the rewrite should still make that claim, just in the user's voice.
- **Don't fake typos or errors.** Humanisation ≠ introducing mistakes. Real humans write cleanly too; the signal is voice and structure, not errors.
- **Don't ignore the register.** Casual voice in formal documents is the biggest failure mode. Always reconcile voice and register.
