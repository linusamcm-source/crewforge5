# Register pack: email

Internal or external email. Split on audience:

- **Internal peer/team** → exemplar: Register 1 in [../business-register-exemplars.md](../business-register-exemplars.md). Full "fine at most registers" AU idiom ("quick one", "sort out", "happy to", "reckon" allowed). Personality welcome.
- **External client/partner** → exemplar: Register 2 in the same file. Safest idiom only ("happy to", "keen to", "across"); drop "reckon", "no worries", "chuck".

**Conventions**
- Lead with the subject, never "I hope this finds you well".
- Specifics over abstractions: numbers, slide references, dates.
- Close with concrete options, not "let me know your thoughts".
- Greeting and sign-off from the voice profile if attested ("Hey team —", "Cheers, L").

**Tell emphasis**: greeting padding, "upon reviewing", "please do not hesitate", hedge-stacked requests.
