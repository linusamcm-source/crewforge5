# Register pack: casual message

Slack, DM, text, WhatsApp — short messages to peers or mates.

**Conventions**
- Match the profile's casual-only markers verbatim: lowercase starts, dropped full stops, fragments, abbreviations — whatever the profile actually shows.
- Short. If the rewrite is longer than the draft, something went wrong.
- No sign-off unless the profile has one for this channel.
- Full AU idiom range from the profile, including "reckon", "no worries", "arvo" if attested.

**Cross-dock rule**: drafts under ~50 words skip the tells.sh baseline and the Step 7 verify gates — one-pass rewrite, deliver. A two-line Slack reply can't absorb a full three-layer pipeline.

**Tell emphasis**: greeting padding, closing offers ("Let me know if you have any questions!"), any corporate verb at all.

**Exemplars**: the user's own recent messages ARE the exemplar. Profile casual samples outrank everything in this file.
